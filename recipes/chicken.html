Garlic Chicken recipe

Home

Description

Garlic Butter Pan-Seared Chicken Breasts feature tender, juicy chicken cooked in a skillet to achieve a golden crust, then basted in a rich, savory garlic butter sauce and garnished with fresh parsley. It is a quick, high-protein meal that pairs easily with roasted vegetables, rice, or pasta.

ingredient

  • Boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • Butter
  • Olive oil (for searing)
  • Minced garlic
  • Fresh parsley (chopped, for garnish)
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Paprika or preferred poultry seasoning (for the golden crust)

Steps

  1. season: Coat chicken with salt, pepper, and paprika.
  2. sear: Cook in olive oil over medium-high heat for 5–6 minutes per side.
  3. baste: Add butter and minced garlic to the pan; spoon melted butter over chicken for 1–2 minutes.
  4. serve: Top with fresh parsley, rest 5 minutes, and slice.
